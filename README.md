# Finance Tracking

Finance Tracking is an Android app for keeping receipts, recording expenses, and understanding where your money goes.

## What you can do

- Add expenses and capture receipt details from photos with AI assistance.
- Search saved receipts and review their items, totals, and purchase details.
- Explore spending statistics and scroll through your history by year, month, week, day, or hour.
- Use English, Greek, or your phone's language, and adjust the theme and text size.
- Export and import CSV files, or create a password-protected backup of receipts and settings.
- Check for app updates from Settings → Updates.
- Share the app using the QR code in Settings → About.

## Install or update

Open [the latest release](https://github.com/0Shard/finance-tracking-releases/releases/latest) and download its `.apk` file. Open it on your Android phone and approve installation. Android may first ask you to allow installation from the app opening the file.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. To update, install the new APK over the existing app. Do not uninstall first: your saved receipts and settings remain in place during an update.

The app can check for updates without a GitHub account. Optional background checks notify you about new versions; you choose when to download and approve installation.

## Your data

Saved receipts are stored on your phone. AI receipt processing sends the content you submit to the configured AI provider and requires your own API key. Full backups are password-encrypted and include saved receipts, settings, and that API key; keep both the backup and its password safe. CSV exports are not encrypted. Original gallery photographs are not included in backups.
